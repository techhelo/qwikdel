How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your QwikBill account and associated data by contacting us using the email address below.

  1. Send an email from, or include, the email address associated with your QwikBill account.
  2. Use the subject line QwikBill Account Deletion Request.
  3. Tell us that you want your QwikBill account and associated data deleted.
  4. We may verify account ownership before processing the request.

support.jktechdevelopers@gmail.com

Important: Replace the email address above with your actual QwikBill support/privacy email before publishing this page.

Data That Will Be Deleted

Subject to applicable legal and security requirements, account deletion may include:

  • QwikBill account information and authentication-related account data.
  • Business/store profile information.
  • Products and inventory information.
  • Bills and invoice history associated with the account.
  • Customer information stored in the account.
  • Device-binding information associated with the account.
  • Other account-associated data that is not required to be retained.

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained when required by applicable law, tax or accounting requirements, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Where retention is required, the information will be retained only for the period necessary for the applicable purpose and will be handled according to our privacy and security practices.

Digital Invoice Links

Previously shared public digital invoice links may continue to be accessible for a limited period while deletion is being processed or where retention is legally required. We will take reasonable steps to remove account-associated digital invoice data when it is eligible for deletion.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after the request and account ownership have been verified.

If additional information is required to verify the request, we may contact you using the information provided with the request.

Local Data on Your Device

QwikBill may store cached information locally on your device. After your account is deleted, you should also uninstall QwikBill or clear its app data if you want locally stored information removed from your device.

Questions

For privacy questions or requests concerning your QwikBill account, contact the QwikBill support/privacy team using the email address provided above.
